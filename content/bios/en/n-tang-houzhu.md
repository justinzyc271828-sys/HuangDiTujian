# Li Yu, Last Ruler of Southern Tang · Major Events

Li Yu was the last ruler of the Southern Tang. In the second year of the Song Jianlong era (961), the Middle Ruler Li Jing died, and Li Yu succeeded to the throne at Jinling, with Southern Tang already at a disadvantage against the Song. This page takes 《宋史·卷478·南唐世家》 as the chronological backbone, cross-checked against the annals in 《宋史·太祖纪》 (available in the local reference library).

Around the Kaibao era (971), he renounced the imperial title, styled himself Ruler of Jiangnan, and submitted to the Song as a vassal in search of a precarious peace; throughout his reign he poured his energy into painting, calligraphy, music, and verse, and his literary achievement far outran his record as a sovereign.

In the seventh year of Kaibao (974), Cao Bin and others led the Song army south against Jiangnan and brought it to the banks of the Yangtze; in the eighth year (975) Song forces breached Jinling, the Last Ruler came out and surrendered, and Southern Tang fell. This campaign grew out of the Jiangnan strategy of [[n-song-tai-zu|Emperor Taizu of Song]]'s court and can be read against the Song-side basic annals.

In the ninth year of Kaibao (976) he was moved north to Bianjing and enfeoffed as the Marquis of Weiming; in confinement his ci poems dwelled on longing for his lost realm. He died at the capital in the third year of Taiping Xingguo (978), while Emperor Taizong of Song was on the throne; a folk legend holds that poison was granted to him on the Double Seventh night, but the standard histories record little, and the matter is treated here as unverified.

His afterlife lies in literature: the ci of the Two Rulers are an entryway into the history of the genre, his failure as a sovereign is read apart from his achievement as a poet, and later judgment has often carried a measure of sympathy. Read alongside Southern Tang Yuanzong (the Middle Ruler Li Jing), the page shows the shape of a Southern Tang that ran its course in two generations, from the Middle Ruler to the Last Ruler.
