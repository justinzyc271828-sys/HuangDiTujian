# Zhao Huan, Emperor Qinzong of Song · Major Deeds

Zhao Huan, eldest son of [[n-song-hui|Emperor Huizong]], received the abdication in 1125 as Jin armies advanced, and changed the era to Jingkang.

In 1126 the Jin besieged Kaifeng. [[li-gang|Li Gang]] organized the defense and rejected peace terms, but was soon sidelined by the chancellors; the court veered between war and peace, and relief armies lost their chance through indecision. In the eleventh month the city fell. In 1127 the Jin carried off both emperors with the imperial clan - the end of Northern Song. Zhang Bangchang's puppet regime lasted weeks before collapsing.

Qinzong was taken north, enfeoffed Duke of Tianshui, and died in Jin lands in 1161. His younger brother Kang Wang [[s-song-gao|Zhao Gou]] was enthroned at Yingtian, beginning the Southern Song.
