# Zhu Qizhen, Emperor Yingzong of Ming (Tianshun) · Major Deeds

In 1457 the Restoration coup brought [[ming-ying-zong-a|Yingzong]] back to the throne, era Tianshun.

That very day he executed Yu Qian in the market, to the grief of the realm. He trusted the Restoration men Shi Heng and Cao Jixiang, who rose against him in turn - the Cao-Shi revolt - and were crushed. His later years were quiet; on his deathbed in 1464 he decreed an end to the custom of burying concubines with the emperor - a practice kept for six reigns, abolished at his word.

Twice enthroned, misled first by a eunuch and then by ministers, he was redeemed by that single act of mercy.
