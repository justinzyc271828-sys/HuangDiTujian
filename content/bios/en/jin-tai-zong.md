# Wanyan Wuyimai, Emperor Taizong of Jin · Major Deeds

Wanyan Wuyimai (1075-1135), younger brother of [[jin-tai-zu|Emperor Taizu]], succeeded him in 1123, era Tianhui.

Taizong's reign saw Jin's explosive expansion: in 1125 his general Wanyan Loushi captured Tianzuo and Liao fell; in 1127 Zonghan and Zongwang marched south, took Kaifeng, and carried off both Song emperors - the Jingkang incident that ended Northern Song. With two capitals taken, Jin stretched from the Heilong River to the Huai, suddenly the strongest power in East Asia.

Taizong also began the Sinicization - official system and examinations. He died in 1135; Taizu's grandson Wanyan Dan succeeded as [[jin-xi-zong|Emperor Xizong]].
