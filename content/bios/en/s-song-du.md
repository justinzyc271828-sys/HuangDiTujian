# Zhao Qi, Emperor Duzong of Song · Major Deeds

Zhao Qi, nephew of [[s-song-li|Emperor Lizong]], was made heir and ascended in 1264, era Xianchun.

For all ten years of his reign power lay with [[jia-si-dao|Jia Sidao]]: state affairs were settled in the chancellor's mansion while the emperor lived deep in the palace, lost in wine and women. In 1268 the Mongols besieged Xiangyang and Fancheng; relief was weak, and the defender Lü Wenhuan held for five years. In 1273 Xiangyang fell and Lü surrendered; the gateway of the Yangtze-Han region was flung open, and the doom of Southern Song was sealed.

He died in 1274; his son Zhao Xian became [[s-song-gong|Emperor Gongzong]].
