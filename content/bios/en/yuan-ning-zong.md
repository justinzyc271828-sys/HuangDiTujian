# Emperor Ningzong of Yuan (Irinchibal) · Major Deeds

Borjigin Irinchibal (1326-1332), second son of Mingzong. When Wenzong died in 1332, his deathbed decree returned the throne to Mingzong's line; El Temur raised Irinchibal at Dadu.

Ningzong reigned barely forty days and died without changing the era, the Zhishun years still running. After his death El Temur would have raised a son of Wenzong but was bound by the decree; in the end Toghon Temur, Mingzong's eldest son, was brought from Guangxi to the throne as [[yuan-hui-zong|Emperor Huizong]].

A child sovereign raised and taken in a flash - a moment in the wake of the Tianli storm.
