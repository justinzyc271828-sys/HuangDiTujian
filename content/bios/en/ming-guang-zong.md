# Zhu Changluo, Emperor Guangzong of Ming · Major Deeds

Zhu Changluo (1582-1620), eldest son of [[ming-shen-zong|Emperor Shenzong]] by a palace maid, spent fifteen years in the succession struggle against the Consort Zheng faction before he was made heir.

When Shenzong died in 1620, Changluo took the throne, planning the Taichang era. He distributed the treasury to the armies and repealed the mining taxes, to general acclaim - then fell ill within the month, took the "Red Pill" of Li Kezhuo, and died overnight: the Red Pill case, one of the three great palace mysteries of the late Ming.

An emperor of one month; the Taichang era never ran. The dynasty by then was sliding toward 1644.
