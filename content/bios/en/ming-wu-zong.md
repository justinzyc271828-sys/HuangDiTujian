# Zhu Houzhao, Emperor Wuzong of Ming · Major Deeds

Zhu Houzhao (1491-1521), only son of [[ming-xiao-zong|Emperor Xiaozong]], took the throne in 1505, era Zhengde.

Wuzong loved pleasure, favored the eunuch Liu Jin and the "Eight Tigers," and after Liu Jin's execution lived in the Leopard House, styling himself "Grand Generalissimo." In 1517 he faced the Tatar prince in person at Yingzhou; in 1519, when the Prince of Ning rose at Nanchang, he led a personal campaign, arriving at Nanjing after Wang Yangming had already quelled it.

He died in the Leopard House in 1521, without issue. Notorious for dissipation, he yet fought in person and put down rebellion.
