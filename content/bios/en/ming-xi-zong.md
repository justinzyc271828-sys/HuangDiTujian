# Zhu Youxiao, Emperor Xizong of Ming · Major Deeds

Zhu Youxiao (1605-1627), eldest son of [[ming-guang-zong|Emperor Guangzong]], took the throne in 1620, era Tianqi.

Xizong loved carpentry and left the government to the eunuch Wei Zhongxian, who styled himself "Nine Thousand Years." The eunuch faction destroyed the Donglin men - Yang Lian, Zuo Guangdou, and others dying in prison - and in 1625 all the academies were razed, the fiercest proscription of the late Ming. On the border, Yuan Chonghuan's victory at Ningyuan in 1626 wounded Nurhaci and steadied Liaodong.

He died in 1627; his younger brother [[ming-si-zong|Zhu Youjian]] succeeded as Sizong.
