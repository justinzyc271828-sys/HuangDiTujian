# Zhao Shu, Emperor Yingzong of Song · Major Deeds

Zhao Shu, son of Prince Pu Anyi, succeeded [[n-song-ren|Emperor Renzong]] in 1063. Renzong's three sons had died young, and in his last years he raised a clan child in the palace - that child was Shu, entering the throne from the imperial clan.

Yingzong reigned only four years (1063-1067), and the entire reign was consumed by the "Puyi Dispute": whether to honor his birth father Prince Pu as imperial father. Censors and ministers fought for years; in the end Prince Pu was honored. Ostensibly a ritual question, it was in fact a clash between two principles of kinship - "serving as heir" versus "honoring the birth father" - played out through Song factional politics.

Strong-willed but ailing, Yingzong died in 1067; his eldest son Zhao Xu became [[n-song-shen|Emperor Shenzong]].
