# Yelü Zongzhen, Emperor Xingzong of Liao · Major Deeds

Yelü Zongzhen (1016-1055), eldest son of [[liao-sheng-zong|Emperor Shengzong]], ascended in 1031, era Jingfu, then Chongxi.

Xingzong loved letters - poetry, painting, calligraphy - but governed flatly. In 1042, taking advantage of the Song-Xia war, he pressed Song for the ten Guannan counties and received increased tribute instead. In 1044 he campaigned against Western Xia in person; the Hequ campaign came to nothing. His later years bred suspicion against his brother Chongyuan, leaving a succession fault line.

He died in 1055; his eldest son Yelü Hongji became [[liao-dao-zong|Emperor Daozong]]. Liao's slide from its peak began under Xingzong.
