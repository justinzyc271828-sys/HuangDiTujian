# Wanyan Jing, Emperor Zhangzong of Jin · Major Deeds

Wanyan Jing (1168-1208), grandson of [[jin-shi-zong|Emperor Shizong]], ascended in 1189, era Mingchang.

Zhangzong carried Jin's Sinicization furthest: rites and music codified, offices fixed, examinations flourishing - the cultural summit of Jin. The Taihe war of 1206 ended in victory; Song delivered Han Tuozhou's head and increased tribute in the Jiading Treaty.

But extravagance crept in with the culture - palaces and rewards beyond measure, a draining treasury - and to the north the Mongols were rising unseen. Jin turned from peak to decline under Zhangzong. He died in 1208 without issue; his uncle Wanyan Yunji succeeded as [[jin-wei-shao|Prince Weishao]].
