# Minning, Emperor Xuanzong of Qing (Daoguang) · Major Deeds

Aisin Gioro Minning (1782-1850), second son of [[qing-ren-zong|Emperor Renzong]], took the throne in 1820, era Daoguang.

His thirty years saw the state weak and the door still shut: in 1839 Lin Zexu burned the opium at Humen; in 1840 the Opium War began and the Qing lost battle after battle; in 1842 the Treaty of Nanjing ceded Hong Kong and opened five ports - the first of the unequal treaties. Daoguang himself was frugal to austerity, but blind to the wider world and without means to resist.

He died at Beijing in 1850. A thrifty emperor at the great turn, the old empire's sickness now plainly visible.
