# Wanyan Shouxu, Emperor Aizong of Jin · Major Deeds

Wanyan Shouxu (1198-1234), third son of [[jin-xuan-zong|Emperor Xuanzong]], ascended in 1223, era Zhengda.

Aizong, the last Jin emperor, is remembered as diligent and frugal, striving to restore the state - but the Mongol tide was beyond turning. In 1232 the Mongols besieged Bianjing; starving, he broke out and fled to Caizhou. In 1234 Song-Mongol forces converged on the city; as it fell, Aizong hanged himself at Youlan Pavilion. Jin perished.

From Aguda's founding in 1115 to this day, one hundred and twenty years. Aizong's death was not a wastrel's end but a diligent last sovereign's martyrdom to the age - "of all emperors past, none knew the grief of a fallen state as I do," his last words ran.
