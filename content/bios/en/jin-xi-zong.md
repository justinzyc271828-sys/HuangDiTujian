# Wanyan Dan, Emperor Xizong of Jin · Major Deeds

Wanyan Dan (1119-1149), eldest grandson of [[jin-tai-zu|Emperor Taizu]], succeeded after [[jin-tai-zong|Emperor Taizong]]'s death in 1135.

Xizong, schooled in Chinese classics from youth, pushed Sinicization: the Tianjuan reforms of 1138 issued a Chinese-style bureaucracy with the Six Ministries under a Secretariat; in 1141 the Shaoxing Treaty made Song a vassal along the Huai with annual tribute, fixing the Song-Jin equilibrium for decades.

His later years dissolved into wine and killing - nobles and ministers executed without measure, the court in terror. In 1149 Wanyan Liang killed him and took the throne as [[jin-hailiing|Prince Hailing]].
