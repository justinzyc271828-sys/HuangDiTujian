# Emperor Yingzong of Yuan (Shidibala) · Major Deeds

Borjigin Shidibala (1303-1323), son of Renzong, took the throne in 1320, era Zhizhi.

Young and purposeful, he made Baiju chancellor and launched the Zhizhi reforms: cutting superfluous posts and expense, tightening discipline, and drafting the comprehensive code. The reforms struck at entrenched interests; in 1323 the censor-in-chief Tieshi and others rose at Nanpo and killed the emperor and Baiju - the Nanpo coup.

Yingzong died at twenty-one after four years of rule. His unfinished reforms ended the Yuan's bid at renewal, and rapid succession and ministerial usurpation followed.
