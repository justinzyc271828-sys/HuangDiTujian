# Xuanye, Emperor Shengzu of Qing (Kangxi) · Major Deeds

Aisin Gioro Xuanye (1654-1722), third son of [[qing-shi-zu|Emperor Shizu]], took the throne at eight in 1661; seizing the regent Oboi in 1669, he ruled in person.

His reign was one of arms and reach: the Three Feudatories rose in 1673 and were crushed in eight years by generals (the emperor did not campaign); in 1683 Shi Lang took Taiwan and a prefecture was set there; against Galdan he campaigned in person - in 1696, leading the army beyond the passes, his general Fiyanggú routed Galdan at Jao Modo and the Khalkha submitted; the Zunghar realm was broken. In 1689 the Treaty of Nerchinsk with Russia fixed the northeastern frontier.

He died at Beijing in 1722 after sixty years on the throne - unrivalled in the Qing in war and governance, "the greatest emperor."
