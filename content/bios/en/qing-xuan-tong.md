# Puyi, Emperor Xuantong of Qing · Major Deeds

Aisin Gioro Puyi (1906-1967), son of the regent Zaifeng and nephew of [[qing-de-zong|Emperor Dezong]], took the throne at three in 1908, era Xuantong, with his father as regent.

In 1911 the Wuchang Uprising spread province by province; in February 1912 the abdication edict ended the imperial system. The Republic granted the court favorable terms and Puyi remained in the Forbidden City. His later life touched the Zhang Xun restoration of 1917 and the Manchukuo puppet state (1934-1945); pardoned in 1959, he died in Beijing in 1967.

Emperor at three, abdicated at six, swept by the age. From Shunzhi's entry in 1644, the Qing had run 268 years - and with Puyi's abdication, the Chinese imperial system itself came to an end. This atlas closes with the Xuantong emperor.
