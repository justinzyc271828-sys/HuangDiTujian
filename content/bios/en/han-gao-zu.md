# Emperor Gaozu of Han · Principal Deeds

Liu Bang entered the turmoil of the late Qin from the career track of a Sishui village head (tingzhang), becoming the Duke of Pei and then King of Han before finally ascending as emperor. The route of his life is almost a map of "how an imperial vacuum is refilled": raising troops at Pei County, entering the Pass with his legal covenant, the Chu-Han stalemate, and fixing the capital in Guanzhong.

Read in parallel with [[qin-shi-huang|Qin Shi Huang]]: one man completed the first large-scale unification and shaped the imperial institution; the other rebuilt a more sustainable empire on the wreckage of that institution and the ruins of war. Read against [[qin-er-shi|Qin Er Shi, the Second Emperor of Qin]]: one can see how "central paralysis" pushed local strongmen onto the stage.

This MVP page sets up only the skeleton; the details of the Chu-Han struggle, the group of meritorious ministers, and events such as Baideng can be added to the timeline in M1/M2.
