# Wanyan Liang, Prince Hailing of Jin · Major Deeds

Wanyan Liang (1122-1161), grandson of [[jin-tai-zu|Emperor Taizu]], killed [[jin-xi-zong|Emperor Xizong]] in 1149 and took the throne, era Tiande.

Brutal and suspicious, Hailing was nonetheless capable: he moved the capital to Yanjing (Zhongdu), razed the old palace at Huining, pushed Sinicization and institutional reform, and built a vast fleet and army to "unify all under heaven." In 1161 he led a colossal campaign against Song; Yu Yunwen defeated him at Caishi and the crossing failed. Mutiny broke out and his own generals killed him near Yangzhou.

With Hailing dead, Wanyan Yong was enthroned at Liaoyang as [[jin-shi-zong|Emperor Shizong]]. Hailing's cruelty undid him, and the southern war bled Jin of strength.
