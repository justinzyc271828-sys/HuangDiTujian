# Emperor Tianshun of Yuan (Arigaba) · Major Deeds

Borjigin Arigaba (1320-1328), son of Emperor Taiding. When Taiding died at Shangdu in 1328, Dawlat Shah and others enthroned Arigaba there, era Tianshun.

Meanwhile El Temur raised Tugh Temur at Dadu; two capitals, two emperors - the Two-Capital War. The Liaodong-Shangdu armies marched south and were beaten; Shangdu fell, and Tianshun vanished from history, taken or lost.

He reigned little more than a month. The Yuan History grants him no biography; legitimacy ran through the Dadu line, and this atlas admits him as a quasi-emperor.
