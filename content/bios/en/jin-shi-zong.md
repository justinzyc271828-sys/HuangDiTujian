# Wanyan Yong, Emperor Shizong of Jin · Major Deeds

Wanyan Yong (1123-1189), grandson of [[jin-tai-zu|Emperor Taizu]], was enthroned at Liaoyang in 1161 after [[jin-hailiing|Hailing]]'s murder, era Dading.

Shizong curbed luxury, prized farming, lightened punishment, and rested the people; the Longxing Treaty of 1164 changed the Song relation to uncle-nephew with halved tribute, quieting the border. He preserved Jurchen ways while absorbing Han institutions, and the Jin called him "Little Yao and Shun."

He died in 1189; his grandson Wanyan Jing succeeded as [[jin-zhang-zong|Emperor Zhangzong]]. The Dading governance was Jin's peak - and, quietly, the beginning of its decline.
