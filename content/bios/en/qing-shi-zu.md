# Fulin, Emperor Shizu of Qing (Shunzhi) · Major Deeds

Aisin Gioro Fulin (1638-1661), ninth son of [[qing-tai-zong|Emperor Taizong]], took the throne at six in 1643 under the regency of Dorgon and Jirgalang.

In 1644 Dorgon led the army through the passes, defeated Li Zicheng, and fixed the capital at Beijing (the boy emperor did not campaign; the regent commanded). The Qing then ruled the Han by Han institutions - examinations restored, the new court taking shape. From 1651, after Dorgon's death, Shizu ruled in person: he disciplined officialdom, curbed the Manchu nobles, and leaned toward Chinese governance.

He died at Beijing in 1661 at twenty-four (a legend of abdication to a monastery persists). Posthumously Shizu, buried in Xiaoling.
