# Mongke Khan, Emperor Xianzong of Yuan · Major Deeds

Borjigin Mongke (1209-1259), eldest son of Tolui, was famed from Batu's western march. After three vacant years, the kurultai of 1251 raised him to the khaganate.

Stern and disciplined, he purged the Ogedei-line rivals and restored khagan power. In 1253 he sent his brother Hulegu west while campaigning in person to destroy Dali, taking the southwest; in 1258 he led the armies against Song into Sichuan, besieging Hezhou and Diaoyucheng.

In 1259, before the unconquered fortress of Diaoyucheng, Mongke died in camp - by cannon wound or illness, as the histories differ. His death dissolved the Song campaign and opened the struggle between his brothers Khubilai and Ariq Boke.
