# Emperor Wuzong of Yuan (Qaishan) · Major Deeds

Borjigin Qaishan (1281-1311), nephew of Chengzong and Prince Huaining, commanded the northern army and had crushed the Qaidu revolt. When Chengzong died in 1307 he entered the capital with his army and took the throne, era Zhida.

Wuzong won the throne by force and dispensed it by whim: titles and bounty flowed without measure to princes and ministers, the treasury drained, and his "Zhida notes" unsettled the currency. With his brother Ayurbarwada ([[yuan-ren-zong|Emperor Renzong]]) he made the pact of brother-to-brother, nephew-from-nephew succession - the seed of a generation of succession strife.

He died in 1311; his brother Renzong honored the pact, then broke it by passing the throne to his own son.
