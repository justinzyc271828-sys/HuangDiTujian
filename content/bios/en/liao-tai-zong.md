# Yelü Deguang, Emperor Taizong of Liao · Major Deeds

Yelü Deguang (902-947), second son of [[liao-tai-zu|Emperor Taizu]], was raised to the throne by Empress Shulü's decision.

His greatest achievement was the acquisition of the Sixteen Prefectures: in 936 he campaigned in person to destroy Later Tang and enthroned Shi Jingtang as his "son emperor," receiving the Sixteen Prefectures of Yan-Yun in return with annual tribute. The northern gate of the Central Plains swung open; Liao held the passes and settled the pattern of a century of Song-Liao relations. In 946 he destroyed Later Jin and entered Bian, changing the dynastic title to Liao - but his soldiers plundered the countryside, alienating the people, and he had to turn back north, dying at Luancheng on the way.

Under Taizong the dual administration matured, governing Han lands by Chinese law; Liao's Sinicization deepened. After his death Empress Shulü raised his nephew Yelü Ruan, [[liao-shi-zong|Emperor Shizong]].
