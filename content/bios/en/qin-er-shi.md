# Qin Er Shi, the Second Emperor of Qin · Principal Deeds

Huhai took the throne after the sudden death of [[qin-shi-huang|Qin Shi Huang]], and his reign was brief. The main line presented by the histories is this: decisions made deep within the palace were commandeered by powerful ministers, punishments grew still harsher, and this fed a vicious cycle with the flames of revolt east of the passes. For this atlas, the value of the Second Emperor's page lies in:

1. showing how the information density of a "short-reigned emperor" is organized;  
2. reading the succession relationship against the First Emperor's page;  
3. serving as a springboard for the narrative from the fall of Qin to the uprising of [[han-gao-zu|Emperor Gaozu of Han]].

The main text and timeline still rest on standard accounts such as the *Shiji*; disputed details are treated with restraint, pending "historiographical notes" to be added alongside in a later version.
