# Yelü Jing, Emperor Muzong of Liao · Major Deeds

Yelü Jing (931-969), son of [[liao-tai-zong|Emperor Taizong]], took the throne in 951 after [[liao-shi-zong|Shizong]]'s assassination, era Yingli.

Muzong reigned nineteen years, remembered as "loving wine and long sleep, careless of state affairs": he missed court, drank day and night, and let government decay. During his reign Song-Liao relations held in equilibrium while Northern Han leaned on Liao against Zhou. In 969 his attendants, exasperated by his brutality, killed him at the Huaizhou palace.

His reign contrasts sharply with that of [[liao-jing-zong|Jingzong]], who restored order and shared power with Xiao Chuo - evidence of what Muzong's years had cost.
