# Zhu Youcheng, Emperor Xiaozong of Ming · Major Deeds

Zhu Youcheng (1470-1505), son of [[ming-xian-zong|Emperor Xianzong]] by a palace maid named Ji, was hidden in the palace as a child from Consort Wan's malice. He took the throne in 1487, era Hongzhi.

Xiaozong was the only Ming emperor with a single wife, no concubines ever. He employed Liu Jian, Xie Qian, and Li Dongyang, heard remonstrance, cleared the transmitted offices, and curbed the eunuchs - the Hongzhi years were the cleanest of the middle Ming.

He died in 1505. His only son [[ming-wu-zong|Wuzong]] undid much of it, but the Hongzhi example stood.
