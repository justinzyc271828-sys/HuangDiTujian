# Hong Taiji, Emperor Taizong of Qing · Major Deeds

Aisin Gioro Hong Taiji (1592-1643), eighth son of [[qing-tai-zu|Emperor Taizu]], took the throne in 1626, era Tiancong.

He changed the khagan title to emperor and the state's name: in 1636 the Later Jin became Qing and he assumed the imperial title, era Chongde. Ruling the Han by Han institutions, he set up the Three Inner Academies and the Six Ministries and honored Confucian learning, completing the institutional preparation for the conquest. In 1636 he campaigned in person against Joseon; King Injo surrendered and the kingdom passed from Ming vassalage to Qing. Twice his armies broke through the Great Wall, raiding the capital region and Shandong, wearing the Ming out.

He died at Shengjing in 1643, posthumously Taizong. He never entered the passes, but everything necessary for entering them was his work.
