# Zaichun, Emperor Muzong of Qing (Tongzhi) · Major Deeds

Aisin Gioro Zaichun (1856-1875), only son of [[qing-wen-zong|Emperor Wenzong]], took the throne at six in 1861 under the two empresses dowager, Cixi and Ci'an.

In 1864 Zeng Guoquan took Nanjing and the Taiping were crushed; the Nian rebels fell in turn. With rebellion quelled, the Self-Strengthening began - the Zongli Yamen, the Jiangnan Arsenal, the Fuzhou shipyard - the "Tongzhi restoration." But the boy emperor ruled in name only; within two years of taking power himself he died, traditionally of smallpox, without issue.

He died in 1875, posthumously Muzong. The chance of renewal died with him.
