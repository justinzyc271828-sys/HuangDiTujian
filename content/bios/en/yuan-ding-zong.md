# Guyuk Khan, Emperor Dingzong of Yuan · Major Deeds

Borjigin Guyuk (1206-1248), eldest son of Ogedei, had earned renown in Batu's western march. After Toregene's four-year regency, the kurultai of 1246 raised him to the khaganate.

Guyuk reigned only two years: he sent envoys to the papal court, opening east-west contact, yet his feud with Batu ran deep, and he was riding west to settle it when he died at Qum-Sengir in 1248.

His death left the throne vacant three years as the princes maneuvered, until Mongke of the Tolui line was raised. Guyuk's brief reign and the ensuing struggle foreshadowed the empire's division.
