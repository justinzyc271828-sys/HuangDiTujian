# Yelü Hongji, Emperor Daozong of Liao · Major Deeds

Yelü Hongji (1032-1101), eldest son of [[liao-xing-zong|Emperor Xingzong]], ascended in 1055, era Qingning.

Daozong reigned forty-six years, the longest in Liao. He put down Chongyuan's revolt in 1063; in the Dakang years he trusted Yelü Yixin, who framed Empress Xiao Guanyin to death and destroyed Crown Prince Jun - the "Yixin catastrophe" that cut off the line of succession. Daozong revered Buddhism, feeding monks and building temples without measure, and honored Confucian learning besides; yet offices were bought, discipline decayed.

He died in 1101; his grandson Yelü Yanxi succeeded as [[liao-tianzuo|Emperor Tianzuo]]. The roots of Liao's fall were already in Daozong's reign.
