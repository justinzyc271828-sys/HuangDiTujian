# Zhao Bing, Emperor Bing of Song · Major Deeds

Zhao Bing, son of [[s-song-du|Emperor Duzong]], held the title Prince Wei. After [[s-song-duan|Duanzong]] died at Naozhou in 1278, Zhang Shijie and Lu Xiufu enthroned Bing, era Xiangxing, stationed at Yashan (modern Xinhui, Guangdong).

In 1279 the Yuan general Zhang Hongfan attacked Yashan; the Song fleet was annihilated. Lu Xiufu carried the child emperor into the sea, and tens of thousands followed into death. The Song perished - 320 years from Taizu's founding in 960. Under the low clouds of Yashan, a civilization's memory sank into the southern sea.
