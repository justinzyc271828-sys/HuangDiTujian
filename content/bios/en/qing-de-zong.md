# Zaitian, Emperor Dezong of Qing (Guangxu) · Major Deeds

Aisin Gioro Zaitian (1871-1908), son of Prince Chun, took the throne at four in 1875 under the regency of Empress Dowager Cixi.

In 1898 he and Kang Youwei launched the Hundred Days Reform; within a hundred days Cixi crushed it, confined the emperor to Yingtai, and executed the Six Gentlemen. In 1900 the Boxers rose; the Eight Powers took Beijing and the court fled west to Xi'an; the Boxer Protocol exacted 450 million taels. Guangxu never governed in fact again and died in grief.

He died at Beijing in 1908, one day before Cixi herself (the cause disputed). He would have reformed but was bound by the empress - the tragedy of the dynasty's end.
