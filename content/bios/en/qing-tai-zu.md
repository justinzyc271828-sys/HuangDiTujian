# Nurhaci, Emperor Taizu of Qing · Major Deeds

Aisin Gioro Nurhaci (1559-1626), chieftain of the Jianzhou Jurchen, lost his father and grandfather to Ming hands and rose with thirteen suits of armor. He united the Jianzhou, then the Haixi tribes, and created the Eight Banner system - soldier and farmer one - the foundation of the Later Jin.

In 1616 he took the khagan title at Hetu Ala, founding the Later Jin with the Tianming era (the name "Qing" came only with Taizong's change in 1636). In 1619, at Sarhu, he routed the Ming's four columns in person, deciding the fate of Liaodong; in 1621 he took Shenyang and Liaoyang, moving the capital to Shenyang.

In 1626, attacking Ningyuan, he was wounded by Yuan Chonghuan's cannon and died on the way home. His heirs completed the unification he had begun and entered the passes.
