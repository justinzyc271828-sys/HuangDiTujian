# Wanyan Xun, Emperor Xuanzong of Jin · Major Deeds

Wanyan Xun (1163-1223), grandson of [[jin-shi-zong|Emperor Shizong]], was raised by Heshilie Zhizhong in 1213 after [[jin-wei-shao|Weishao]]'s murder, era Zhenyou.

In 1214, with the Mongols besieging Zhongdu again, Xuanzong abandoned the sixty-year-old capital and moved south to Bianjing - the "Zhenyou retreat." Hemmed in on three sides (Mongols north, Western Xia west, Red-Jacket rebels east), he chose to raid Song annually "to take compensation from the south," squandering the last neighborly goodwill.

He died at Bian in 1223; his third son Wanyan Shouxu succeeded as [[jin-ai-zong|Emperor Aizong]]. The retreat lost the root, the raids lost the friend - Jin shrank further.
