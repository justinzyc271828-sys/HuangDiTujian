# Yelü Yanxi, Emperor Tianzuo of Liao · Major Deeds

Yelü Yanxi (1075-1128), grandson of [[liao-dao-zong|Emperor Daozong]] and son of Crown Prince Jun, ascended in 1101, era Qiantong.

Tianzuo hunted, ignored counsel, and let government rot - precisely when the Jurchen Wanyan clan was rising. In 1114 [[jin-tai-zu|Wanyan Aguda]] revolted against Liao; at Ningjiangzhou, Chuhedian, and beyond, the Liao armies were smashed one after another. The Central and Southern Capitals fell; Tianzuo fled west into the mountains and was captured at Yingzhou in 1125. Liao fell.

Reduced to a marquis by the Jin, he died in 1128. The Jurchen rise owed much to Aguda's strength, but more to Liao's decay - a state's collapse begins within.
