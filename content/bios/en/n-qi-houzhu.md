# The Last Sovereign of Northern Qi · Principal Deeds

Gao Wei, styled Rengang, was the eldest son of Wucheng. In the 1st year of Tiantong Wucheng abdicated and Wei took the throne at ten, changing the era to Tiantong. The Last Sovereign "loved the pipa," and composed a *Song without Sorrow* which he played and sang himself while hundreds of attendants joined in — the histories call him the "Sorrowless Sovereign": making the realm's ruin a joy without sorrow, his blindness could go no further.

Twelve years he reigned, and Qi went from decline to death: in the 3rd year of Wuping, on the slanders of Zu Ting and the rest, he slew the great general Hulü Guang — "the realm's shield, gone in a day," to the joy of Zhou; he also slew the Prince of Lanling, Gao Changgong, and other princes and generals, pulling down his own wall. In the 7th year of Wuping Emperor Wu of Zhou campaigned in person; Jinzhou fell, the Last Sovereign fled to Ye in panic, and passed the throne to the infant Gao Heng; in the 1st year of Chengguang the Zhou army took Ye, the Last Sovereign was captured, and Northern Qi was no more.

Taken to Chang'an, he was made Duke of Wenguo by Emperor Wu of Zhou, and soon put to death on a charge of "revolt." The histories say: "the Sorrowless Sovereign, the sovereign of a lost realm." The enterprise of Gao Huan and Gao Yang, six sovereigns and twenty-eight years, was spent — Wucheng's license and the Last Sovereign's blindness pulling down the wall until the realm fell. "Without sorrow" became the prophecy of Qi's ruin.

> References: the punctuated critical edition of the *Bei Qishu*, ch. 8 ("Benji of the Last Sovereign").
