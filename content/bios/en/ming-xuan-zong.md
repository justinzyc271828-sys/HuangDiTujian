# Zhu Zhanji, Emperor Xuanzong of Ming · Major Deeds

Zhu Zhanji (1399-1435), eldest son of [[ming-ren-zong|Emperor Renzong]], took the throne in 1425, era Xuande.

In 1426 the Prince of Han revolted at Le'an; Xuanzong campaigned in person and crushed him in one move. He then resolutely abandoned the Jiaozhi commandery, ending distant ventures to husband the state's strength. With the Three Yangs - Yang Shiqi, Yang Rong, Yang Pu - guiding him, he governed diligently, and the Xuande age is counted the summit of Ming prosperity.

He died in 1435, his son succeeding. He departed at the peak; the decline that followed marks his reign as the turning point.
