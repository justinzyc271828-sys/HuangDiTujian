# Genghis Khan, Emperor Taizu of Yuan · Major Deeds

Borjigin Temujin (1162-1227), son of the Qiyan chieftain Yesugei, lost his father in childhood and grew up in desperate hardship beside the Onon River, captured once by the Tayichiud and surviving by his wits. Through endurance and cunning he gathered the scattered tribes, defeating the Merkit, Tatar, Khereid, and Naiman in turn to unite the steppe.

In 1206 the kurultai at the source of the Onon raised him to the great khaganate with the title Genghis Khan. Then came the great campaigns: in 1211 he marched on Jin in person and annihilated its elite at Yehuling, raiding Hebei and Hedong year after year; in 1219 he led the western war that destroyed Khwarazm, while Jebe and Subutai crossed the Caucasus and swept Central Asia and the southern Rus.

Returning from the west, he attacked Western Xia in 1226 and destroyed it in 1227, dying in camp at Liupanshan that year. His deathbed strategy - passing through Song territory to destroy Jin - guided his heirs. The empire he founded was the beginning of the largest contiguous realm in world history.
