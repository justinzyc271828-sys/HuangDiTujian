# Emperor Mingzong of Yuan (Khuslen) · Major Deeds

Borjigin Khuslen (1300-1329), eldest son of Wuzong. By the brother-succession pact he should have followed his father, but Renzong broke the pact and passed the throne to his own son; Khuslen fled west and lived years in the Chagatai khanate.

In 1329 Wenzong abdicated in his favor; Khuslen was enthroned on the road to Karakorum and made Wenzong heir. That August, at a banquet at Wanghuchadu, he died suddenly at thirty - widely believed poisoned by El Temur on Wenzong's behalf, though nothing was ever proved.

A reign of half a year. His son Toghon Temur ([[yuan-hui-zong|Emperor Huizong]]) later took the throne and stretched the Yuan's life another three decades.
