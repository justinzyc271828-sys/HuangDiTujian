# Zhu Jianshen, Emperor Xianzong of Ming · Major Deeds

Zhu Jianshen (1447-1487), eldest son of [[ming-ying-zong-b|Emperor Yingzong]], endured the Tumu years and deposition as a child. He took the throne in 1464, era Chenghua.

He began well, clearing Yu Qian's name; but he doted on Consort Wan, whose favor dominated the court, set up the Western Depot under the eunuch Wang Zhi, and created the "transmitted appointments" - offices granted by palace fiat without examination, opening the door to purchased rank.

He died in 1487. Under his favor of eunuchs and consorts, the middle-Ming decay began.
