# Yizhu, Emperor Wenzong of Qing (Xianfeng) · Major Deeds

Aisin Gioro Yizhu (1831-1861), fourth son of [[qing-xuan-zong|Emperor Xuanzong]], took the throne in 1850, era Xianfeng.

His eleven years burned with troubles within and without: in 1851 Hong Xiuquan rose at Jintian and the Taiping spread across the southeast, fixing their capital at Nanjing; in 1856 the Second Opium War began; in 1860 the Anglo-French took Beijing, burned the Yuanming Yuan, and the emperor fled to Rehe, leaving Prince Gong to sign the Beijing Convention.

He died at the Rehe retreat in 1861. Enthroned in disaster, dead in exile, he left behind the regency of Cixi and Ci'an that ruled in Tongzhi's name.
