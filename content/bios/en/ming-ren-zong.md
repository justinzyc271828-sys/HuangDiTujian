# Zhu Gaochi, Emperor Renzong of Ming · Major Deeds

Zhu Gaochi (1378-1425), eldest son of [[ming-cheng-zu|Emperor Chengzu]], was mild by nature and often regent during his father's northern campaigns. When Chengzu died at Yumuchuan in 1424, Gaochi took the throne, era Hongxi.

He opened with benevolence: the western voyages were halted, taxes lightened, the Jianwen loyalists pardoned, Yang Shiqi and others called to office - the realm settled. He reigned only ten months, dying at forty-eight.

Brief as it was, his benevolence underlay the Ren-Xuan peace, the most tranquil years of early Ming.
