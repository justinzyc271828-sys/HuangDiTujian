# Emperor Zhongzong of Tang (Restored) · Principal Deeds

Li Xian, restored in the 1st year of Shenlong. In Empress Wu's last years, Zhang Jianzhi, Cui Xuanwei, Jing Hui, Huan Yanfan, and Yuan Shuji staged the Shenlong coup, forced Wu to abdicate, and restored Zhongzong; the realm reverted to Tang — the fifteen years of the Wu Zhou were at an end. He honored Wu as "Grand Saintly Emperor Zetian," and she died soon after; the capital returned to Chang'an.

But he was "restored and unable to revive the court": the Wu remnants like Wu Sansi held power, linking with Empress Wei; the Princess Anle "sold offices by slanted letters," and the government fell into disorder. In the 1st year of Jinglong the heir Li Chongjun rose, slew Wu Sansi, and fell and died. Five years on the throne, every matter decided by Empress Wei — "the realm resented it."

In the sixth month of the 4th year of Jinglong Zhongzong died suddenly in the Shenlong Hall, aged fifty-five — the histories suspect Empress Wei and the Princess Anle of poison. Wei took the regency and set up the Prince of Wen, Li Chongmao (Emperor Shang); within a month Li Longji staged the Tanglong coup, slew Empress Wei, and the throne passed back to Ruizong and Xuanzong. Twice on the throne, twice unable to stand: deposed by Wu the first time, ruled by Wei the second — the decline of the Tang house began with Zhongzong.

> References: the punctuated critical edition of the *Jiu Tangshu*, ch. 7 ("Benji of Emperor Zhongzong").
