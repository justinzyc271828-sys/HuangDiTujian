# Emperor Renzong of Yuan (Ayurbarwada) · Major Deeds

Borjigin Ayurbarwada (1285-1320), younger brother of Wuzong, was schooled in Confucian learning from youth. When Wuzong died in 1311 he took the throne by the brother-succession pact, era Huangqing.

Renzong's reign was praised: he restored the examinations in 1313, held the first Yuan session in 1315, commissioned translations of the classics, and pressed the Sinicization of government. But in his later years he broke the pact with his brother, setting aside Wuzong's sons to pass the throne to his own son Shidibala ([[yuan-ying-zong|Emperor Yingzong]]) - the root of the Tianli storms.

He died in 1320. The broken pact ended the Yuan's middle peace.
