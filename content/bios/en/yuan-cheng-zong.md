# Emperor Chengzong of Yuan (Temur) · Major Deeds

Borjigin Temur (1265-1307), grandson of Khubilai, was made heir in 1293 and took the throne at Shangdu after Khubilai's death in 1294.

His thirteen years were consolidation: the Qaidu-Duwa revolt ended in 1301 and the northwest quieted, giving his reign the name of the Dade peace. Yet he granted lavishly to princes and clans, draining the treasury, and in his later years the empress Bulughan meddled in government.

He died in 1307; his nephew Qaishan marched in from the north with the army to take the throne as [[yuan-wu-zong|Emperor Wuzong]].
