# Zhao Shi, Emperor Duanzong of Song · Major Deeds

Zhao Shi, eldest son of [[s-song-du|Emperor Duzong]], held the title Prince Yi. After Lin'an surrendered in 1276 the two princes fled south; Zhang Shijie and Lu Xiufu enthroned Shi at Fuzhou, era Jingyan - the Song cause held on.

His two years (1276-1278) were spent drifting: Wen Tianxiang raised troops in Jiangxi and was soon captured; the court moved by sea through Fujian and Guangdong, caught between land and wave. In 1278 Duanzong died at Naozhou; his younger brother Zhao Bing succeeded as [[s-song-di-bing|Emperor Bing]].
