# Zhao Kuo, Emperor Ningzong of Song · Major Deeds

Zhao Kuo, son of [[s-song-guang|Emperor Guangzong]], was placed on the throne by the Shaoxi abdication in 1194, era Qingyuan.

Ningzong reigned thirty years (1194-1224) yet power never left the hands of ministers. Under Han Tuozhou: in the Qingyuan years Zhu Xi and others were branded "pseudo-scholars" and their learning proscribed - the Qingyuan proscription; in 1206 Han launched the Kaixi campaign, which failed, and Jin demanded his head - in 1208 Song delivered it with increased tribute in the Jiading Treaty. Thereafter Shi Miyuan ruled, and Ningzong reigned without governing.

He died in 1224 without issue; Shi Miyuan raised his nephew Zhao Yun by forged edict, [[s-song-li|Emperor Lizong]].
