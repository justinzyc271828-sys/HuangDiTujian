# Zhao Xu, Emperor Zhezong of Song · Major Deeds

Zhao Xu, sixth son of [[n-song-shen|Emperor Shenzong]], ascended at ten in 1085 under the regency of Empress Gao.

During the Yuanyou years (1086-1093) Sima Guang and the conservatives repealed the reforms wholesale - the "Yuanyou restoration" - then fell to quarreling among themselves. When Empress Gao died in 1093 Zhezong took power himself, revived the Xining policies, and demoted the Yuanyou faction - the "Shaosheng revival." One reign, two opposite governments: "two different men before and after."

He died in 1100 without issue; Empress Xiang raised Shenzong's eleventh son, Prince Duan, to the throne as [[n-song-hui|Emperor Huizong]].
