# Yelü Xian, Emperor Jingzong of Liao · Major Deeds

Yelü Xian (948-982), son of [[liao-shi-zong|Emperor Shizong]], took the throne after [[liao-mu-zong|Muzong]]'s murder in 969, era Baoning.

Frail but purposeful, he employed ministers such as Shi Fang and Yelü Xiuge, and gave his empress Xiao Chuo (Xiao Yanyan) a share in government - a partnership that laid the ground for Shengzong's golden age. In 979, when Emperor Taizong of Song campaigned in person against Youzhou, Jingzong sent Yelü Xiuge and others to meet him; the rout at the Gaoliang River left the Song emperor fleeing for his life, a decisive turn that put Liao on the strategic offensive.

He died at Yunzhou in 982; his eldest son Yelü Longxu succeeded under Xiao Chuo's regency as [[liao-sheng-zong|Emperor Shengzong]].
