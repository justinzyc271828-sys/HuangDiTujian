# Zhao Shen, Emperor Xiaozong of Song · Major Deeds

Zhao Shen, seventh-generation descendant of [[n-song-tai-zu|Emperor Taizu]] and son of Prince Xiu, received the abdication from [[s-song-gao|Emperor Gaozong]] in 1162, era Longxing. Historians call him the finest emperor of Southern Song - the one who most wished to recover the north.

In 1163 he sent Zhang Jun north; the rout at Fuling ended the campaign, and in 1164 the Longxing Treaty changed the Song-Jin relation from vassal to nephew (uncle-nephew rank) with halved tribute. Xiaozong then rebuilt the army and tended to governance; the Qiandao and Chunxi years are praised as an age of order. But the moment never came: the recovery dream went unfulfilled.

In 1189 he abdicated to his third son Zhao Dun, [[s-song-guang|Emperor Guangzong]]; he died in 1194. His posthumous name carries the character for filial piety, honoring his devotion to Gaozong.
