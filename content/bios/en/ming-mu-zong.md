# Zhu Zaihou, Emperor Muzong of Ming · Major Deeds

Zhu Zaihou (1537-1572), third son of [[ming-shi-zong|Emperor Shizong]], Prince Yu, took the throne in 1566, era Longqing.

His six years were energetic: Gao Gong and Zhang Juzheng entered the Grand Secretariat; in 1570, when Altan Khan's grandson surrendered to Ming, the "Altan enfeoffment" followed - Altan was created Prince Shunyi in 1571 with markets opened, and the northern border rested. The maritime ban was also eased in the "Longqing opening."

He died in 1572. His brief good governance prepared Zhang Juzheng's Wanli reforms.
