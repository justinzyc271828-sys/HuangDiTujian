# Wanyan Yunji, Prince Weishao of Jin · Major Deeds

Wanyan Yunji (1168-1213), seventh son of [[jin-shi-zong|Emperor Shizong]], took the throne after [[jin-zhang-zong|Emperor Zhangzong]]'s death in 1208, era Da'an.

His five years coincided with the Mongol rise: in 1211 Genghis Khan swept south and the Jin elite was annihilated at Yehuling and Huihebao, shaking Zhongdu; in 1212 the Mongols besieged the capital and Jin bought respite with peace talks while the court fell apart. In 1213 the strongman Heshilie Zhizhong killed him at his residence and raised [[jin-xuan-zong|Emperor Xuanzong]].

Overwhelmed by a crisis beyond his measure, Weishao marks the point where Jin's collapse became irreversible.
