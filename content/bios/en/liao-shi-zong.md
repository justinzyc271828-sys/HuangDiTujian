# Yelü Ruan, Emperor Shizong of Liao · Major Deeds

Yelü Ruan (918-951), eldest grandson of [[liao-tai-zu|Emperor Taizu]], was raised to the throne by the army when [[liao-tai-zong|Emperor Taizong]] died at Luancheng in 947. Empress Shulü wished her own son Lihu to reign; the court nearly came to arms before she yielded.

Shizong reigned five years (947-951), era Tianlu. He favored Sinicization and Han ministers, and repeatedly campaigned south against Later Han, plundering Hebei. In 951 he was assassinated by the clansman Chage on the way to a sacrifice for his father, at thirty-four - a short reign that mirrored the Liao court's inner strife.
