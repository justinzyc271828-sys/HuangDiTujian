# Emperor Wenzong of Yuan (Tugh Temur) · Major Deeds

Borjigin Tugh Temur (1304-1332), second son of Wuzong, was raised in the south and learned the Chinese letters. When Taiding died in 1328, El Temur raised him at Dadu, era Tianli; the Two-Capital War followed and Dadu won.

In 1329 Wenzong yielded the throne to his elder brother Khuslen ([[yuan-ming-zong|Emperor Mingzong]]), enthroned on the road to Karakorum - only for Mingzong to die suddenly that August at Wanghuchadu, Wenzong resuming the throne at Shangdu. The death was widely charged to El Temur's poison, with Wenzong's knowledge or hand - one of the great mysteries of the Yuan.

His reign of about four years founded the Kuizhang Pavilion, gathered scholars such as Yu Ji and Jie Xisi, and compiled the State Compendium - a genuine culture of letters. He died in 1332, having decreed that the throne return to Mingzong's line; his nephew Irinchibal ([[yuan-ning-zong|Emperor Ningzong]]) followed and soon died.
