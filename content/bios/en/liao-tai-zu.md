# Yelü Abaoji, Emperor Taizu of Liao · Major Deeds

Yelü Abaoji (872-926) of the Diela tribe of the Khitan was the figure who carried the Khitan from tribal confederation to statehood. Having united the tribes, he was raised to the khagan throne in 907, held the office through successive terms, and in 916 assumed the imperial title with era names, founding the state of Khitan (later renamed Liao).

Abaoji relied heavily on Han advisers such as Han Yanhui, who built his institutions and palaces; he created the Khitan script and established the dual administration - northern offices for the Khitan, southern offices for the Han - the institutional cornerstone of the Liao. In 925 he campaigned against Balhae in person and destroyed it the next year, renaming it Eastern Dan under his eldest son.

He died at Fuyu in 926; Empress Shulü settled the succession and his second son Yelü Deguang followed as [[liao-tai-zong|Emperor Taizong]].
