# Zhao Xian, Emperor Gongzong of Song · Major Deeds

Zhao Xian, son of [[s-song-du|Emperor Duzong]], ascended at four in 1274 under Grand Empress Dowager Xie, era Deyou.

In 1275 Jia Sidao took the field at Dingjiazhuang and collapsed at the first clash; the main army was lost and the Yuan armies drove on Lin'an. In 1276 Bayan entered Lin'an; Gongzong submitted and was taken north with the dowagers, reduced to Duke of Ying. The Song cause passed to the fleeing princes [[s-song-duan|Yi]] and [[s-song-di-bing|Wei]].

In the north he took up Buddhism and lived in Tibet; tradition holds he was put to death in 1323. A child who lost a state - history pities him.
