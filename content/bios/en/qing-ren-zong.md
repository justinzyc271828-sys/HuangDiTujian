# Yongyan, Emperor Renzong of Qing (Jiaqing) · Major Deeds

Aisin Gioro Yongyan (1760-1820), fifteenth son of [[qing-gao-zong|Emperor Gaozong]], took the throne by abdication in 1796, under three years of the retired emperor's tutelage.

In 1799, with his father dead, he executed Heshen and confiscated an estate worth 800 million taels - "Heshen fell, Jiaqing ate his fill." But the White Lotus revolt spread through Sichuan, Hubei, and Shaanxi for nine years, costing 200 million taels; the wealth of the splendor was spent on suppression. Frugal and anticorruption as he was, the age was past saving.

He died at the Rehe palace in 1820. A consolidator at the end of an era, willing but unable.
