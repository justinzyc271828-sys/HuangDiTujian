# Yelü Longxu, Emperor Shengzong of Liao · Major Deeds

Yelü Longxu (971-1031), eldest son of [[liao-jing-zong|Emperor Jingzong]], ascended at twelve in 982, with Empress Dowager Xiao Chuo as regent for twenty-seven years.

In 1004 the Liao army marched south in force, with the empress and the young emperor at the front; the resulting Chanyuan Treaty made Song and Liao brothers, with Song paying annual tribute in silver and silk. The treaty brought a century of peace. After the empress's death Shengzong ruled in person, continuing the Sinicization - examinations, codified law, Chinese-style institutions - and Liao reached its zenith, the "Tonghe golden age."

He died in 1031; his eldest son Yelü Zongzhen succeeded as [[liao-xing-zong|Emperor Xingzong]].
