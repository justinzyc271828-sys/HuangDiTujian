# Yinzhen, Emperor Shizong of Qing (Yongzheng) · Major Deeds

Aisin Gioro Yinzhen (1678-1735), fourth son of [[qing-sheng-zu|Emperor Shengzu]] and Prince Yong, took the throne in 1722, era Yongzheng.

His thirteen years were a stern discipline: the Grand Council was founded, drawing confidential business from the Grand Secretariat into the throne's own hands; the single-tax merged the head tax into the land levy, easing the landless; the secret succession ended the heir-apparent storms; and the campaign against corruption was the fiercest of the Qing. The legitimacy of his accession (the alleged tampering with the will) and the severity of his rule have always been debated together.

He died at Beijing in 1735. Stern as he was, his reign is the hinge between Kangxi and Qianlong.
