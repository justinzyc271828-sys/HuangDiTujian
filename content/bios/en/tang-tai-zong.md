# Emperor Taizong of Tang · Major Deeds

Li Shimin secured the realm as Prince of Qin and entered the succession through the Xuanwu Gate Incident; the Zhenguan reign became famous for its employment of talent and acceptance of remonstrance, and left a clear military-geographic track on the northern frontier and in the Liaodong direction. This page draws mainly on the Taizong annals of the two Tang histories and the Tang chapters of the Zizhi Tongjian.

This atlas also includes the key node before his enthronement (the Jinyang uprising), so that the map connects into a single life line running "from Hedong to Chang'an, then north and east". Read alongside [[han-wu-di|Emperor Wu of Han]], one can see the "narratives of zenith" of different eras; read alongside [[qin-shi-huang|Qin Shi Huang]], one can see the differences in governing style within the unified-empire tradition.
