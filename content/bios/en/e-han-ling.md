# Emperor Ling of Han · Principal Deeds

Liu Hong was the son of the marquis Chang of Xieduting. [[e-han-huan|Emperor Huan]] died without issue, and Empress Dowager Dou with Dou Wu raised the twelve-year-old Hong as Emperor Ling — the curtain rises on the last act of Eastern Han.

In the very first year of his reign, Dou Wu and Chen Fan's plot against the eunuchs failed and cost them their lives; the eunuchs struck back, the second proscription began, and six or seven hundred famous men of the realm were barred from office. For the next twenty years the eunuchs and the consorts took turns at power while the emperor turned his attention elsewhere: in the first Guanghe year he opened office sales at the West Garden, with set prices for dukes, ministers, and provincial governors — even the Three Dukes could be bought; in the same year he founded the Hongdu Academy, choosing scholars for calligraphy and letters against the classicists of the Grand Academy; in the second Zhongping year he built the Hall of Ten Thousand Gold to hoard money at the West Garden. Four hundred years of the Han inheritance had become, in Ling's hands, goods with a price tag.

In the first Zhongping year Zhang Jiao of Julu gathered followers through the Way of Great Peace, and the Yellow Turban revolt erupted — "eight provinces rose together, and the realm answered." Ling lifted the proscription to rally the scholars, and Huangfu Song and Zhu Jun suppressed the revolt; but the power of provincial governors grew from that war, and the regional strongmen each held troops of their own. The realm of Han was already coming apart. In the fifth Zhongping year he created the Eight Colonels of the West Garden, among them Yuan Shao and Cao Cao — this imperial guard would become the roster of the coming warlord age.

In the sixth Zhongping year Ling died at thirty-four, leaving an empire in tatters: eunuchs and scholars at each other's throats, Yellow Turban remnants across the provinces, and frontier warlords closing in. [[e-han-shao-bian|The Young Emperor Bian]] was deposed by Dong Zhuo within months, and Eastern Han entered its last age.
