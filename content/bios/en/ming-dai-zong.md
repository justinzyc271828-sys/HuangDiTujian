# Zhu Qiyu, Emperor Daizong of Ming (Jingtai) · Major Deeds

Zhu Qiyu (1428-1457), second son of [[ming-xuan-zong|Emperor Xuanzong]], Prince of Cheng. After the Tumu disaster, with [[ming-ying-zong-a|Yingzong]] in captivity, he was raised from regent to emperor in 1449, era Jingtai, honoring his brother as retired emperor.

He entrusted Yu Qian with the Ministry of War; the defense of Beijing repelled Esen's siege and the realm was saved. In 1450 Yingzong was welcomed back and confined in the Southern Palace; Daizong then deposed the heir, his nephew, in favor of his own son - the seed of the Restoration coup.

In 1457 Shi Heng and Xu Youzhen restored Yingzong; Daizong was reduced to Prince of Cheng and died within a month, some say by murder. He saved the state and wronged his brother; posterity names him Emperor Jing.
