# Emperor Taiding of Yuan (Yesun Temur) · Major Deeds

Borjigin Yesun Temur (1293-1328), grandson of Khubilai and Prince Jin, guarded the northern marches. After the Nanpo coup killed Yingzong in 1323 he took the throne at Shangdu, era Taiding.

His five years were consolidation: he executed the Nanpo conspirators, kept old ministers, and steadied the court. But he governed loosely, and the fiscal rot went uncured.

He died at Shangdu in 1328. His son Arigaba was enthroned there as [[yuan-tian-shun|Emperor Tianshun]] while the Wuzong faction raised Tugh Temur ([[yuan-wen-zong|Emperor Wenzong]]) at Dadu; the Two-Capital War followed, Tianshun fell, and the Yuan's succession wars began in earnest.
