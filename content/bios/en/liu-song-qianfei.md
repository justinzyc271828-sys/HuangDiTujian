# The Former Deposed Emperor · Principal Deeds

Liu Ziye, eldest son of Emperor Xiaowu, took the throne in the 8th year of Daming at sixteen. His one year on the throne made him the worst tyrant of the southern dynasties: he changed the era from Yongguang to Jinghe within a single year; he slew ministers and princes — Dai Faxing, Liu Yigong, and the rest — until court and realm trembled; and his "unbounded license" and obscenities fill the records. The *Nanshi* calls him "wicked, perverse, without an ounce of the sovereign's bearing."

In the eleventh month of the 1st year of Jinghe, the Prince of Xiangdong, Liu Yu (Emperor Ming), with Ruan Dianfu and the others, had Shou Jizhi slay him in the Hualin Park, aged seventeen; Yu took the throne. His tyranny carried the slaughter of the Song house to its height: from Xiaowu on, brothers and uncles and nephews destroyed each other, and by the Deposed Emperor's reign the princes were nearly gone.

The historians see in him not a monster but the product of the southern palace system: since Emperor Wen's slaying and Xiaowu's massacre, kin slaying kin had become the instinct of Liu Song. One year on the throne, and the state never recovered: under Emperor Ming the family slaughters went on, the Later Deposed Emperor was slain, Emperor Shun abdicated — within twenty years Liu Song was gone.
