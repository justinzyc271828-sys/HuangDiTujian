# Zhao Dun, Emperor Guangzong of Song · Major Deeds

Zhao Dun, third son of [[s-song-xiao|Emperor Xiaozong]], received the abdication in 1189, era Shaoxi.

Controlled by Empress Li, he fell out with his father: for years he refused to visit the Chonghua Palace where Xiaozong lived, alarming court and country - the "failure to attend" affair. When Xiaozong died in 1194 Guangzong pleaded illness and the funeral rites nearly failed. Ministers Zhao Ruyu and Han Tuozhou obtained the grand dowager's edict, raised Prince Jia Zhao Kuo to the throne, and made Guangzong retired emperor - the Shaoxi abdication.

He died in 1200. A five-year reign in which household disorder shook the state.
