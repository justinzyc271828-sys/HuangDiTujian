# Wanyan Aguda, Emperor Taizu of Jin · Major Deeds

Wanyan Aguda (1068-1123) of the Wanyan clan united the Jurchen tribes and became their leader. In 1114 he swore the tribesmen to revolt against Liao, took Ningjiangzhou, and routed a hundred-thousand-strong Liao army at Chuhedian. In 1115 he assumed the imperial title, founding the Great Jin with the Shouguo era, and fixed the capital at Huining (modern Acheng, Harbin).

Aguda was famed for winning against overwhelming odds. He opened the war that destroyed Liao, taking the Liao Upper Capital Linhuang in person in 1120 and shaking Liao to its roots. He created the Jurchen script and made the "Sea Alliance" with Song to attack Liao from two sides.

He died on campaign in 1123; his brother Wanyan Wuyimai succeeded as [[jin-tai-zong|Emperor Taizong]]. The conquests of Liao and Song were completed by his heirs, but the foundation was Aguda's.
